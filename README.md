# Industrial Manufacturing Financial Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

An end-to-end financial analysis project for a fictional industrial machinery manufacturer.

The project uses a synthetic dataset of **220 sale orders** to analyze financial performance, profitability, product performance, working capital, cost structure, and business scenarios.

The objective is to understand how manufacturing and sales data can be transformed into actionable financial insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall financial performance
- Measure revenue and profitability
- Compare product-wise financial performance
- Analyze customer revenue contribution
- Evaluate working capital components
- Identify major cost drivers
- Perform scenario and sensitivity analysis
- Support management-oriented financial decision making

## 📈 Analysis Performed

### 1. P&L Analysis
Analysis of:
- Revenue
- Cost of Goods Sold
- Gross Profit
- EBITDA
- Net Profit
- Profit margins

### 2. Product Profitability

Compared industrial machinery models based on:
- Revenue
- Gross Profit
- Gross Margin

**Highest revenue model:** 4-Spindle – ₹22.38 Cr

**Highest gross margin model:** Optima 1100CB – 33.06%

### 3. Customer Analysis

Analyzed customer-wise revenue contribution and identified the highest-value customers.

### 4. Working Capital

Analyzed:
- Raw Material Inventory
- Work in Progress
- Finished Goods
- Receivables
- Payables

### 5. Cost & Scenario Analysis

Tested the potential impact of changes in:
- Material cost
- Selling price
- Labour cost
- Operational efficiency
